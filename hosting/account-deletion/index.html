WeGoVroom Account Deletion

Use this page to request deletion of your WeGoVroom account and associated app data.

How to request account deletion

Email us at wegovroom0191@gmail.com from the email address used for your WeGoVroom account.

Include the subject line: WeGoVroom Account Deletion Request.

What will be deleted

  • Your account profile, including name, email, avatar, and profile details stored by WeGoVroom.
  • Your app-generated user data such as trips, requests, messages, feedback, notifications, and reviews where deletion is technically possible.
  • Your authentication account used to sign in to WeGoVroom.

Data we may retain

We may retain limited records if required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or dispute-resolution purposes. Retained records are kept only as long as necessary for those purposes.

Processing time

We aim to process deletion requests within 30 days after verifying that the request belongs to the account owner.
