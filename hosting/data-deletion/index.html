WeGoVroom Data Deletion

Use this page to request deletion of personal data associated with your WeGoVroom account.

How to request data deletion

Email us at wegovroom0191@gmail.com from the email address used for your WeGoVroom account.

Include the subject line: WeGoVroom Data Deletion Request and mention whether you want specific data deleted or full account deletion.

Data covered by this request

  • Profile information such as name, email, avatar, and profile details.
  • Trip-related data, join requests, chat messages, notifications, feedback, and reviews stored by WeGoVroom.
  • Other personal information linked to your WeGoVroom account.

Data we may retain

We may retain limited records if required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or dispute-resolution purposes. Retained records are kept only as long as necessary for those purposes.

Processing time

We aim to process data deletion requests within 30 days after verifying that the request belongs to the account owner.
